# Cat tracker – Grepow curved cell datasheet checks (2026-11-12)

Sources: Grepow spec sheets `GRP751538-3.85V-440mAh` (Rev A) and `GRP402425-3.8V-225mAh` (Rev A,
2019-07-31), both supplied by the user. Neck-fit numbers are estimates from the 72 mm phantom.

## Corrections to `claude/note_to_integration_2026-11-11.md` §3
- The 440 mAh part number is **GRP751538** (not GRP7515038).
- The 440 mAh cell's max charge voltage is **4.4 V**, not 4.35 V. Capacity is rated 4.4 → 3.0 V.
- The 225 mAh cell (GRP402425) is 4.35 V max, as noted.

## Fit on the neck (estimates)
- The cell's concave face sits at roughly R ≈ 37.6 mm (36 mm phantom + skin + pod wall).
- 440 mAh cell, 38.1 mm chord: a flat cell would stand off ~5.2 mm at its ends. At R52.4 the
  cell's own sag is ~3.6 mm, so the mismatch is about **1.6 mm**. Pod about 17–19 mm wide with
  skin (vs 22.1 now). Radial height about 11–12 mm over the neck with skin (vs ~13.9 for the
  602030). Size the cavity for the 7.50 mm swollen thickness, not 6.94.
- 225 mAh cell: radius unknown. Even flat, a 25 mm chord only stands off ~2.1 mm, and the cell is
  4 mm thick, but it is 24.2 mm wide (wider than the current pod).
- Ask Grepow: the 225's radius, and whether the 440 can be made at R ≈ 37–38 mm (tooling, MOQ,
  price). Also the 440's tab length (it isn't clear on the drawing).

## Charger (nPM1300) – to verify against the PS
- VTERM must not exceed the cell's max: 4.35 V for the 225; up to 4.40 V for the 440.
- Recommended: **VTERM 4.35 V for either cell.** The 440 gives up a few % of capacity, but one
  setting and one protector variant cover both cells, and it already meets the 440's 0–10 °C
  rule (4.35 V). (From memory, the nPM1300 VTERM steps include 4.35 and 4.40 V; check the PS.)
- Cold charging: the 440 needs ≤0.2C (≤88 mA) below 10 °C; the 225 must not charge below 10 °C.
  This needs an NTC at the cell. Bare cells have none; check whether one is on the flex/board.
  Simplest: charge current ≤88 mA always (fine for a dock charge), plus an NTC cut-off at 0 °C
  (440) or 10 °C (225).
- 225 mAh: the charge current must be ≤200 mA.

## Load (225 mAh only)
- Max continuous discharge 100 mA. The +22 dBm LoRa TX (~118 mA at 3.3 V on 3V3_RF) is roughly
  110–120 mA from the cell, plus the MCU and GNSS if they overlap. This is over the continuous
  rating but well inside the 500 mA / 3 s pulse rating for short packets. The 440 has no issue.

## Cell protection on the flex (§4 of the integration note)
- Overdischarge threshold: both cells cut off at 3.0 V; pick ~2.8–3.0 V detect.
- Overcharge threshold: with VTERM 4.35 V on both, a protector with OVP around 4.425–4.45 V
  works for both. If the 440 is charged to 4.40 V, it needs ~4.45–4.475 V OVP, which is too
  high for the 225, so the cells would need different protector variants.
- Overcurrent: set above the TX burst for the 440; the 225's 100 mA continuous rating is below
  most protectors' discharge OCP, so the protector won't enforce it.

## Assembly notes from the sheets
- Tabs: ultrasonic or spot weld preferred. Hand soldering allowed at ≤350 °C, ≤3 s, ≤5 times,
  cooling between. The cell body must not exceed ~100 °C (no heat-cured silicone, as noted).
- Don't bend the sealing edge; bend a tab at most 3 times. Fix the cell by its large face; no
  movement in the pod. Insulate between the wiring and the cell.
- Storage: 3.7–3.9 V long-term, −20 to 35 °C; cycle every 3 months if stored.
